## Introduction

OpenAI Presence is a managed enterprise platform that enables businesses to build, deploy, and continuously improve AI agents within their workflows. ([openai.com](https://openai.com/index/introducing-openai-presence/?utm_source=openai))

## Key Features

- **Trusted AI Agents:**
  - Agents follow approved instructions, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and organizational policies.
  - Connect to business systems through APIs and tools with scoped permissions.
  - Retrieve information, update systems, and complete approved actions.
  - Communicate through supported voice or chat experiences.
  - Escalate to a person or another support path when human judgment is needed.

- **Governance and Control:**
  - Define policies and permissions.
  - Test agent behavior through simulations and evaluations.
  - Monitor production outcomes.
  - Involve people when human judgment is necessary.

- **Continuous Improvement:**
  - Agents improve through real-world experience.
  - Evaluations, guardrails, and human approval govern every change.
  - Use production conversations and quality signals to recommend improvements that make agents more capable.

## Getting Started

1. **Contact OpenAI:**
   - Reach out to your OpenAI account team to discuss eligibility, workflow fit, implementation readiness, and delivery capacity.

2. **Define Your Workflow:**
   - Identify the specific tasks and processes you want the AI agent to handle.

3. **Integration and Deployment:**
   - Work with OpenAI to integrate the agent into your existing systems and deploy it across your workflows.

## Real-World Applications

- **Customer Support:**
  - Build trusted AI support agents grounded in your SOPs, policies, and escalation rules.
  - Resolve common requests across voice and chat, freeing teams for complex cases.

- **Demand Generation:**
  - Engage inbound prospects instantly across voice and chat.

## Pricing and Availability

- **Limited General Availability:**
  - Presence is currently available through limited general availability (limited GA) as a managed deployment.
  - It is not a self-serve product.
  - Access depends on workflow fit, implementation readiness, and available delivery capacity.
  - Contact your OpenAI account representative for more information.

## Conclusion

OpenAI Presence offers a robust solution for businesses looking to integrate AI agents into their workflows. By providing trusted AI agents, governance controls, and continuous improvement mechanisms, Presence enables organizations to enhance efficiency and reliability in their operations.
