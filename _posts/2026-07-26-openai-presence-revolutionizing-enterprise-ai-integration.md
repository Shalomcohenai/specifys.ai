## Introduction

OpenAI's Presence platform, launched on July 22, 2026, is designed to help enterprises deploy and manage AI agents across voice and chat channels. ([openai.com](https://openai.com/index/introducing-openai-presence/?utm_source=openai))

## Key Features

- **Governance and Control**: Presence allows organizations to define policies, permissions, and escalation paths, ensuring AI agents operate within set guidelines. ([help.openai.com](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001405-openai-presence?utm_source=openai))

- **Integration with Business Systems**: The platform connects AI agents to company systems through APIs, enabling them to retrieve information, update systems, and perform approved actions. ([help.openai.com](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001405-openai-presence?utm_source=openai))

- **Continuous Improvement**: Utilizing a Codex-powered feedback loop, Presence analyzes agent behavior and suggests updates, facilitating ongoing enhancement. ([openai.com](https://openai.com/index/introducing-openai-presence/?utm_source=openai))

## Deployment and Availability

Presence is currently available through a limited general availability program, with deployments led by OpenAI Forward Deployed Engineers and select integrators. ([goodiebase.com](https://www.goodiebase.com/news/openai-presence-enterprise-agents-july-2026?utm_source=openai))

## Use Cases

- **Customer Support**: Automate responses to common inquiries, allowing human agents to focus on complex cases. ([openai.com](https://openai.com/business/openai-presence/?utm_source=openai))

- **Demand Generation**: Engage prospects instantly across voice and chat, qualifying leads and capturing intent. ([openai.com](https://openai.com/business/openai-presence/?utm_source=openai))

- **Claims Processing**: Guide customers through the claims process, from intake to resolution. ([openai.com](https://openai.com/business/openai-presence/?utm_source=openai))

## Real-World Application

OpenAI's own English-language phone support service utilizes Presence, with AI agents resolving 75% of calls. ([ai-tldr.dev](https://ai-tldr.dev/releases/openai-presence/?utm_source=openai))

## Getting Started

To explore how Presence can be integrated into your enterprise workflows, contact your OpenAI account representative. ([help.openai.com](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001405-openai-presence?utm_source=openai))

## TL;DR

OpenAI's Presence platform offers enterprises a managed solution for deploying AI agents across voice and chat channels, emphasizing governance, integration, and continuous improvement. To implement Presence, reach out to OpenAI for deployment details.
